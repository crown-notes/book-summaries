# The Unfair Advantage

An unfair advantage is something that puts you ahead of your competition. Each person has their own unique unfair advantages. You can develop more than one unfair advantage or combine yours with those of your friends. The unfair advantages of your friends can complement yours, allowing you both to leverage each other's best abilities.

In this world, nothing will be a fair game. Sometimes you'll win, sometimes you'll lose. The most important part is maintaining a positive mindset, as thinking greatly affects your ability to work. Always have a mindset that is ready to learn.

## The MILES Framework

The authors developed a system called the MILES framework to help individuals identify and refine their unfair advantages.

- **Money:** Having financial resources for education or other needs gives you an edge over others.

- **Intelligence:** Possessing knowledge and the ability to learn quickly about specific topics provides an advantage.

- **Luck:** Being in the right place at the right time can significantly benefit you.

- **Education:** Acquiring knowledge and learning abilities offers an advantage.

- **Status:** Higher status often results in better treatment from others and more willingness to do favors.

## Creating a Startup

Before creating a startup, you must first answer the question: Why are you creating this? Each person will have their own answer to this question. You also need to determine what type of company you'll build: a lifestyle company or a digital product or service.

Investors are interested only in products that can disrupt an industry or have the potential to do so; plan your idea carefully. Not every idea can be turned into a startup. If it’s a completely new concept, you'll have to educate people on how to use your product, and if it's an immediate success, there's a chance that a larger company might offer to buy your product.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> You can't create and manage your company alone. Find someone who wants to work with you and build a team so that you don't have to bear everything on your shoulders.

Conflicts can occur between founders and negatively impact the company. When raising funds, keep the introduction short and simple, and if possible, show investors an MVP of your company.
