# Show your Work

Your content doesn't need to be the best for people to appreciate it. It just needs to be easy to find and relatable to your audience. Be an amateur at many things instead of a master at only one. Diversify your learning.

Our world is changing so rapidly that if you try to master one thing, it might become irrelevant by the time you're done. Publish the work you believe is best, but don't brag about it. The more good work you put out, the more people you will attract; some will like your work, some won't, and some will genuinely offer guidance on improving it.

Life is full of ups and downs. You will have to learn to live with them if you want to fulfill your dreams. Quitting prematurely or just because you aren’t doing well isn’t a valid excuse to discard what you’ve accomplished in your life.

Like a chain-smoker lights a cigarette before the current one finishes, you should also start thinking about your next project before your current one concludes. A creative person’s best quality should be the ability to take criticism positively.

Start learning a new skill and share your progress with others. Write blog posts about your daily learnings and engage in online forums with people pursuing the same interest. Great minds, often regarded as geniuses, have collaborated with like-minded individuals for mutual support and growth.

Creativity flourishes when great minds connect. Engage with those more skilled in your field, learn from them, and contribute to their work. In today's interconnected world, anyone can contribute to anything they desire through the internet.
