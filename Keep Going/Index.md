# Keep Going

Creativity is not a linear process; sometimes you have to look back, and sometimes you have to start again from scratch. Our lives are full of ups and downs, but being creative amidst chaos is possible if you follow a daily routine. A daily routine will keep you occupied and help you enjoy your time.

Sometimes you won't be able to follow your daily routine and will need to do things you never planned on doing. However, the important thing to remember is that you completed some tasks, even if not all.

If you enjoy your hobbies, most people will suggest starting a small business. This is a critical decision because turning a hobby into a business doesn't always work out well. If you really want to make money through your hobbies, consider selling limited amounts. Don't live your life through online metrics; the number of likes and followers matters, but it shouldn't be your only focus.

You need to enjoy the work you do each day. If you don't, then change your process. Learn to accept change for the better and shake things up occasionally. Wake up each day with a goal and work towards completing it. You'll see that people often focus more on their titles than on their work. Forget your title; immerse yourself in your work and let it define you.

Your attention is one of the most important things. In today’s world, everyone wants to steal it from you. There are many things that constantly demand our attention. Learn to give it to what matters most to you.

If there is too much chaos around you, turn on airplane mode and disconnect from the world. Focus on your work, and when you're done, simply turn off airplane mode.

There are only a few things you can focus on at a time, so you need to determine what is most important and what you should decline. Paying too much attention can cause you to miss the tiny details you're looking for.

Take your time with your craft; creativity requires time. Slow down and enjoy the process. People often obsess over the future, but revisiting the past occasionally helps keep your progress in check.
